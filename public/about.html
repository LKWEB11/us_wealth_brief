Who We Are

US Wealth Brief is a fully automated financial news platform dedicated to bringing Americans the latest market news, economic updates, stock market movements, cryptocurrency developments, and investment insights — all in one place.

We believe that access to quality financial information should not be behind a paywall. Every American deserves to know what is happening on Wall Street, in Washington, and across the global economy that affects their savings, investments, and financial future.

What We Do

Our proprietary automated system continuously monitors the most trusted financial news sources in the United States, including:

  • Yahoo Finance — Market data and financial analysis
  • CNBC — Breaking business and financial news
  • MarketWatch — Stock market news and data
  • Bloomberg Markets — Global financial intelligence

Our system aggregates and curates the most important stories and publishes them in a clean, easy-to-read format — updated twice daily so you always have the freshest news.
